# Planning roadmap

This is a proposed **relative 90-day plan**, not a dated commitment or assertion of completed work.

## Days 1–30: frame and investigate

- Refine learner and parent segments.
- Map competitors and alternative learning paths.
- Interview target audiences with consent.
- Define proposed learning outcomes and prototype modules.
- Draft brand, operational and safeguarding standards.
- Prepare evidence and decision logs.

**Gate:** identifiable user problem, coherent segment, preliminary demand signals and no unresolved critical safeguarding blockers.

## Days 31–60: prototype and test

- Produce selected lesson plans, mentor guides and assessment rubrics.
- Build low-risk sample projects and device setup guides.
- Run observed prototype sessions or demonstrations.
- Measure learning friction, instructor workload and equipment requirements.
- Test willingness to pay without exaggerated claims.

**Gate:** acceptable baseline learning experience, safe delivery and credible cost estimates.

## Days 61–90: pilot and decide

- Run bounded pilot cohorts with defined goals and permissions.
- Collect learning evidence, parent feedback, costs, issues and attendance.
- Evaluate the hypotheses against predeclared criteria.
- Revise modules and delivery processes.
- Decide whether to iterate, expand, pause or stop.

**Gate:** teaching quality, learner benefit, safety, operational capacity and basic economics justify the next step.

## Critical dependencies

Instructor competence, usable facilities or remote setup, guardian consent, privacy procedures, reliable learner support and sufficient budget must be resolved before scaling.
