---
title: "Learning Paths"
description: "Draft pathways organized by readiness and learning outcomes."
---

## Explorers — systems thinking

For learners beginning with digital literacy, problem-solving, logic, safe online behavior, and tangible STEM projects. Examples include simple circuits, computational thinking, and visual programming.

## Builders — development foundations

For learners ready to use text-based programming, basic command lines, version control, electronics, and collaborative projects. Suggested topics include Python, Linux concepts, Git, and sensors.

## Makers — applied AI and robotics

For learners comfortable with foundational programming. Suggested topics include APIs, model evaluation, local AI tools, microcontrollers, and supervised robotics projects.

## Advanced — infrastructure and agentic systems

For technically prepared learners. Suggested topics include Ubuntu environments, Docker, local model serving, structured data pipelines, tool-using agents, evaluation, and security.

<Warning>These are draft pathways, not enrollment offerings. Specific prerequisites, durations, equipment, assessment and supervision rules require pilot validation.</Warning>
