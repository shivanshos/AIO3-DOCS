# Quiet foundations. Warm signal.

Ink and Paper carry the identity. Charcoal builds depth. Energy is a controlled visual signal; Energy Deep supports accent text on light surfaces. These are brand colors, not a replacement for product interaction colors.

## Core palette

Ink #161717; Charcoal #282A2B; Paper #F5F4EF; Neutral #A6AAAA; Energy #DA5A38; Energy Deep #B8431F. Energy Glow #E8704F is available for accent text on dark surfaces.

## Semantic use

Use text-primary-light = Ink, text-primary-dark = Paper, surface-default-light = Paper and surface-default-dark = Ink. Neutral is secondary text on dark backgrounds only.

## Accent discipline

Reserve Energy for graphics and large type on Paper. Use Energy Deep for normal-size accent text on Paper. Pair color with a text label or icon when communicating state.

## Gradient boundary

The supplied ember gradient belongs to Studio hero surfaces. Keep logos monochrome and readable. Product UI retains its existing Carbon interactive blue; do not infer new UI state colors from brand artwork.
