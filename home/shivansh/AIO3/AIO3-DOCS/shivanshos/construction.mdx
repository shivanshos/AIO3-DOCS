# Give the identity room.

The FINAL standard sets clear space to half the O height. Treat the O in the selected artwork as the measuring unit and preserve that space on every edge, including beside photographs and navigation.

## Measuring space

Let H be the visible O height. Reserve at least 0.5H around the artwork. Measure visible artwork rather than the SVG canvas; transparent padding is not a reliable reference.

## Size and rendering

Start with the kit minimum widths: seal 24 px, mark 56 px, wordmark 120 px, lockup 180 px. Check at actual rendered size and normal zoom, with both standard and high-density displays.

## Print production

No certified millimeter minimum, CMYK conversion or Pantone match is supplied. Make substrate-specific proofs before committing to signage, fabric, vinyl or metal.

## Never alter geometry

Do not stretch, rotate, outline, redraw strokes, replace the S, add shadows or apply a gradient to the mark. Keep the original aspect ratio. The older 25% symbol / 20% lockup spacing proposal is superseded here.
