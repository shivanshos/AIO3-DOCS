# From system to surface.

The supplied application set demonstrates Studio hero, project and workspace cards, social banners, open-graph images, business cards and brand boards. Every preview should remain labeled Concept until its underlying claims are validated.

## Studio hero

Use the ember surface as the dominant expressive moment, with a monochrome wordmark and restrained supporting text. Keep a clear reading zone and avoid placing small text on variable contrast areas.

## Digital formats

The supplied social banner is 1500 × 500 and the OG image is 1200 × 630. Respect safe margins and test platform crops. Do not assume those crops preserve the full composition.

## Print applications

Business cards are concept proofs. Confirm final dimensions, bleed, trim, substrate and color profile with the printer. Do not imply the raster preview is a press-ready file.

## QR reference

The user-supplied QR image is retained unchanged as a reference. Its destination has not been verified in this delivery. Do not use it as a published navigation or printed action until scan and destination checks are complete.
