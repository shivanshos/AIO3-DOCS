## Photography

Prefer candid Indian students and educators in genuine making environments: notebooks, electronics, computers, labs, prototypes and collaborative learning. Natural daylight, tactile surfaces and respectful representation beat aspirational stock gestures.

Four approved photographic treatments:
1. **Natural:** warm, truthful colour and human skin.
2. **Teal duotone:** Off Black to Sky; analytical and technical stories.
3. **Warm duotone:** Terra Cotta to Apricot; mentorship and belonging.
4. **Monochrome:** simple documentary emphasis.

### Graphic grammar
- Sparse dot and asterisk fields as ambient texture.
- Fine grid lines for technical content.
- Topographic contour lines for discovery and progress.
- Minimal node networks for systems and research.
- Soft radial turquoise/Apricot glows behind, never on, the logo master.

Keep nonessential graphics muted, typically in the original 18–45% opacity range. Leave safe areas for overlaid headings and captions. No robots, brain clip-art, circuits-as-wallpaper, clutter, fake text or unlicensed real-person likenesses.

### Imagery integrity
Label AI-generated imagery in the asset catalogue; do not depict fictional classrooms, mentors, students or outcomes as documentary evidence. For minors, require the applicable consent, privacy and review procedures before publishing recognisable portraits.

### Composition
Recommended variants: 16:9 hero, 4:5 portrait, 1:1 campaign tile. Compose with generous negative space and restrained edge detail.
