## Visual direction: quietly futuristic

High whitespace, graph-paper and node motifs, technical microtypography, a restrained teal glow, responsive modular grids, deliberate motion and authentic local context. Futurism comes from clarity and craft, not visual noise.

## Never change

- Logo artwork, letterforms or proportions
- Locked palette HEX values
- Typography roles
- The 32 px icon grid, 2 px stroke and maximum elevation of 3
- Light/dark theme support, accessibility and reduced-motion support

## Never do

- Generate, redraw or approximate the AIO3 logo with AI
- Use neon gradients, glossy robots, brains, cyberpunk purple or fake text
- Claim guaranteed jobs, income, admissions or validated outcomes
- Publish identifiable minors without guardian consent
- Distribute proprietary fonts without verified licences
