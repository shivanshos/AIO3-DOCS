## Design idea: Clarity as a superpower
Our future-facing quality comes from legible systems, exact geometry and meaningful motion, not sci-fi clichés.

### Core structure
- **The original A3 monogram and AIO3 wordmark:** Preserve approved SVG paths, letterforms and proportions.
- **Research field:** Light dot grids, layered networks, asterisks and topographic contours; avoid distracting backgrounds.
- **Human evidence:** Natural imagery of building, mentoring and experimentation. Use recognizable real people only with permission.

### Composition
Use generous negative space and confident asymmetric editorial grids. Approximate distribution is 70% calm paper/ink surfaces, 20% turquoise accents and 10% warm supporting tones.

### Surfaces and shape
- Buttons and controls: 8 px rounding.
- Cards: 16 px rounding.
- Immersive hero panels: 24 px rounding.
- Pills/chips: fully rounded.
- Elevation: restrained two-layer shadows, never heavier than level 3.

### Motion
Durations: 100, 160, 240, 400 and 600 ms. Animation must explain state or relationships and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Anti-patterns
No neon gradients, glossy robots, brains, cyberpunk purple, invented lettering, generic corporate handshakes or illegible low-contrast overlays.
