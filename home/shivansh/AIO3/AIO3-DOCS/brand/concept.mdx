AIO3 is an **India-first, premium, mentorship-led AI and STEM skill-development studio** in its discovery stage. It helps children and other learners understand, use, build and critically evaluate technology through fundamentals-first, project-based learning.

<Note>
  **Stage:** discovery and validation. The concept below is a working charter (draft v0.1, October 2026), not a validated business case. Offerings, audiences and outcomes remain hypotheses until piloted.
</Note>

## The problem we see

Much technology education teaches the latest interface or tool. When the tool changes, the learner starts again. AIO3 is built on the belief that durable capability comes from understanding the systems underneath: networks, operating systems, programming, data and engineering thinking.

## The idea

> **Learn the fundamentals. Understand the systems. Build what's next.**

AIO3 connects computing foundations with practical work, so learners can explain what their systems do rather than reproduce a demonstration.

## Learning domains

AIO3 intends to cover, where developmentally appropriate:

- Computational thinking and digital literacy
- Programming (including Python) and development environments
- Linux and infrastructure
- AI, automation and agentic workflows
- Electronics and physical computing
- Robotics and drone/UAV fundamentals
- Design, scientific inquiry and responsible technology use

Learning depth follows **demonstrated readiness, not age alone**. See [Learning paths](/learning-paths) for the draft Explorers, Builders, Makers and Advanced pathways.

## Who it is for

| Audience | What they need from AIO3 |
| --- | --- |
| Learners (children, teenagers, adults returning to technology) | Real understanding, practical confidence and documented projects |
| Parents and guardians | Transparent objectives, safety, supervision and honest progress evidence |
| Educators and mentors | A clear framework, rubrics and responsible practice |
| Learning partners | Credible, evidence-led collaboration |

## How it is delivered (proposed)

- Small-cohort and one-to-one mentorship (a hypothesis, not established demand)
- Guided lessons, independent builds, demonstrations and project portfolios
- A physical or hybrid studio, deferred until the model is validated

## What AIO3 is not

- Not a job-guarantee, income or admissions scheme
- Not a promise of instant expertise or automatic academic success
- Not a validated national rollout or a licensed partnership network
- Not "PhD-mentored for every learner"
