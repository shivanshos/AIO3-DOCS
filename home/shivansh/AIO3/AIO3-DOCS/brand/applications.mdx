# Brand applications

## Web and product
Calm editorial hero, honest explanation of outcomes, responsive learning cards, consistent tokens, conversational composer, source-aware answers, accessible navigation and light/dark modes.

## Learning experience
Course maps prioritise readiness, verified skills and meaningful projects. Infrastructure, Linux, Python, AI, robotics and drone learning should be introduced safely and progressively—not sold as guaranteed expertise after short sessions.

## Physical studio
Restrained wayfinding, durable materials, readable signage, clear workshop safety information, meaningful equipment imagery and modular teaching zones.

## Parents and educators
Use human explanations, progression evidence, safeguarding language and documented learning objectives. Less jargon; more transparent demonstrations.

## Marketing
Avoid fake endorsements, fabricated success claims and overprocessed AI imagery. Demonstrate genuine learner work with permission.

## Documentation
Mintlify pages and repository guidelines share one source for the identity, with documented versions and controlled assets. Publishing requires accessibility, spelling, font-rights and preview checks.
