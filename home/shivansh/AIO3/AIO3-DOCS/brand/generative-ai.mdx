Image generators create **only scene, texture, background and mood**, ideally with no text. Real logos and typography are applied afterwards in Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop or Adobe Express.

<Warning>
  Never ask a generative model to create, redraw or "improve" the AIO3 wordmark or A3 icon. Never present generated people, classrooms, facilities, data or outcomes as real.
</Warning>

## Creative brief (paste into any tool)

> Brand: AIO3, always capitals. India-first, premium, hands-on AI/STEM learning studio. Research-minded, learner-first, infrastructure-literate and mentorship-led. Core words: intelligence, curiosity, building, integrity, discovery, human mentorship, technical mastery. Palette: Off Black #091717; Inky Blue #13343B; Peacock #2E565D; True Turquoise #20808D; Bright Turquoise #20B8CD; Sky #BADFDE; Paper White #FBFAF4; Ecru #E5E3D4; Apricot #FFD2A6; Terra Cotta #A94B30; Boysenberry #954456. Design language: editorial Swiss grid meets human-centred lab journal, restrained technical glow, very fine contour, grid and network patterns. No text, no logos, no neon gradients, no glossy robots, no fake text, no clutter.

Always leave a clear area for editorial copy.

## Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate a clean plate">
    Use the brief and an approved prompt. Generate textless backgrounds only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clean up">
    Remove pseudo-text, fake interface labels, invented numbers and logo-like shapes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Composite">
    In Figma or Adobe, add the official outlined AIO3 SVG and correctly licensed typography. Grade to the palette.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record">
    Save the layered master and reviewed exports. Label the asset as AI-generated in the catalogue and preserve Content Credentials where appropriate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Run the [Brand review checklist](/brand/review-checklist) before publishing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review criteria

- Approved palette only
- Safety and respectful representation
- Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 where copy overlays the image
- Clean plate: no pseudo-text or fake data
- Semantic meaning: graphics relate to the content
- Rights and derivative terms checked
