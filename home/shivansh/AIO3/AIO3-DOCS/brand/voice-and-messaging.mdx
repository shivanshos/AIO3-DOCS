## Voice

AIO3 sounds **calm, ambitious, thoughtful, precise and genuinely enthusiastic**. We explain difficult things without talking down to anyone.

| Trait | In practice |
| --- | --- |
| Curious | Invite questions and visible experimentation |
| Precise | Distinguish proposals, pilots and evidence-backed outcomes |
| Empowering | Show a path from fundamentals to independent making |
| Responsible | Make safety, privacy and supervision visible |

## Tone by audience

| Audience | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learners | Encouraging, concrete | "Open the terminal. Let's see what the computer is actually doing." |
| Parents | Reassuring, transparent | "Each module lists its objectives, supervision needs and what your child will build." |
| Educators | Structured, rigorous | "Every module defines prerequisites, evidence of learning and a reflection." |
| Partners | Factual, measured | "AIO3 is in discovery. Here is what we are testing and how." |

## Writing the name

- Always **AIO3**: uppercase, no spaces, no hyphen.
- Never "Aio3", "AIO 3", "A.I.O.3" or "aio3" in public copy (lowercase is fine in URLs and file names).
- Do not explain or expand the name in public copy until an official meaning is documented.

## Claims policy

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Allowed">
    - Describe intent: "AIO3 aims to..." or "We are piloting..."
    - Describe structure: objectives, prerequisites, project types
    - Share real learner work, with permission and consent
    - Cite evidence with source, date and method
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Not allowed">
    - Guaranteed jobs, income, admissions, certification or competition awards
    - "Instant expertise" or mastery after short sessions
    - Fabricated testimonials, endorsements or results
    - Unverified partners, accreditations or national rollout
    - "Every learner is PhD-mentored"
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Vocabulary

| Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| learner, readiness | "kids" (in formal copy), age-only labels |
| build, test, explain, reflect | master, crush, dominate |
| fundamentals, systems | hacks, shortcuts |
| mentorship, guidance | coaching for guaranteed results |
| pilot, draft, proposed | launched, proven (unless true) |
| responsible AI, safe experimentation | AI magic, future-proof |

## Copy mechanics

- Sentence case for headings and buttons.
- Short sentences; one idea per sentence.
- Use plain-language explanations before technical terms.
- Hindi and other Indian-language copy is proofread by native-language reviewers.
- Prompt composer placeholder: **Ask AIO3 anything…**
- Label AI-generated content and distinguish retrieved evidence from generated explanation.

## Example messaging blocks

AIO3 helps learners understand the systems behind modern technology, then build with them, guided by mentors.

**Parent-facing**
Your child learns how technology works, not just how to use it. Every project is supervised, age-appropriate and explained, and we share what was actually learned.

**Short description (under 160 characters)**
India-first AI and STEM studio. Fundamentals first, project-based, mentor-led learning.
