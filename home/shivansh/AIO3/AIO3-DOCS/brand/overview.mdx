## Fundamentals first. Futures built.

AIO3 is an India-first, discovery-stage AI and STEM skill-development studio. The brand exists to make complex technology approachable through rigorous fundamentals, experiments, projects and mentorship. It is **not** a job-guarantee scheme or a promise of specific educational outcomes.

The identity should feel **intelligent, human, quietly futuristic and credible**. The original AIO3 visual mark, its approved variants, typography and palette are the starting point, not raw material for a replacement logo.

### Brand promise
Build capability by understanding the systems beneath modern technology. Learners should be able to explain, build, test and reflect—not merely reproduce a demonstration.

### Audience
Students across readiness levels, parents, educators and technical mentors. Communicate with warmth and clarity rather than dense jargon or artificial prestige.

### Brand voice
- **Curious:** invite questions and visible experimentation.
- **Precise:** distinguish proposals, pilots and evidence-backed outcomes.
- **Empowering:** show a path from fundamentals to independent making.
- **Responsible:** make safety, privacy and appropriate supervision visible.

### Visual guardrails
Do not redraw the original AIO3 wordmark with image-generation models. The approved A3 mark and hexagonal concept remain canonical; alterations to their geometry require explicit review. Add futuristic detail using layout, restrained grid/contour/network graphics, photographic treatment and subtle motion instead.

<Note>Design references and token files are maintained in the private [AIO3-BRAND repository](https://github.com/shivanshos/AIO3-BRAND). Public communications must not imply a validated national rollout, licensed partnerships or guaranteed outcomes.</Note>
