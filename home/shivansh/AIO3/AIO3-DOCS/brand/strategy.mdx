## Positioning

**For** curious learners and the families and educators who support them, **AIO3 is** an India-first, mentorship-led AI and STEM studio **that** builds durable technical understanding through fundamentals, projects and honest feedback. **Unlike** tool-first courses, AIO3 teaches the systems beneath modern technology so capability outlasts any single app.

<Note>
  This positioning describes intent. Geography, learner segment and offer are still undecided in the AIO3 decision log and require interviews and pilots.
</Note>

## Design principle

Modern without trend-chasing. Futuristic without neon sci-fi. Technical without alienating parents or beginning learners. Honest, calm, bold and distinctly AIO3.

## Core words

**Intelligence · curiosity · building · integrity · discovery · human mentorship · technical mastery**

Use these words to judge creative work. If a piece does not express at least two of them, revisit it.

## Personality

| AIO3 is | AIO3 is not |
| --- | --- |
| Calm and confident | Loud or hype-driven |
| Precise | Vague or jargon-heavy |
| Warm and human | Cold or corporate |
| Ambitious | Boastful or exclusive |
| Quietly futuristic | Sci-fi or cyberpunk |
| Evidence-led | Promise-led |

## Audiences and what each needs to feel

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Learners">
    Curiosity and capability. Show real making: notebooks, electronics, terminals, prototypes. Speak to readiness, never to labels.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Parents and guardians">
    Trust and clarity. Lead with learning objectives, supervision, safeguarding and realistic outcomes. Less jargon, more transparent demonstration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Educators and mentors">
    Rigour and support. Provide frameworks, rubrics and responsible-practice guidance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Learning partners">
    Credibility. Never imply partnerships, accreditation or rollouts that do not exist.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Brand architecture

AIO3 is a single masterbrand. Sub-programmes and learning paths sit beneath it as descriptive names, not separate brands.

| Level | Example | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Masterbrand | AIO3 | Master wordmark or A3 icon |
| Workstream | Curriculum, Technical Lab, Research, Mentorship | Plain-language name set in Nohemi; no separate logo |
| Learning path | Explorers, Builders, Makers, Advanced | Descriptive label; no separate logo |
| Project repositories | AIO3-DOCS, AIO3-BRAND, AIO3-Core, AIO3-APP | Internal names only |

Do not create sub-logos, mascots or lockups for programmes without an asset-level brand decision recorded in governance.

## Differentiators to communicate (once evidenced)

1. Fundamentals that outlast tools
2. Build-led learning with explainable projects
3. Small-group and one-to-one mentorship
4. Responsible technology: safety, privacy and consent by design
5. Readiness-based progression

Communicate a differentiator publicly only when there is evidence behind it. See [Research & evidence](/research/overview).
