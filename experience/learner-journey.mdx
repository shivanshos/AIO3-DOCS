# Experience blueprint

The AIO3 experience should feel personal, structured, and credible from first contact through completion.

## Seven stages

| Stage | Learner-facing experience | Operations output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Understand what AIO3 teaches | Accurate public brief |
| Fit assessment | Discuss goals and baseline experience | Readiness notes with consent |
| Onboarding | Understand tools, schedules and safety | Account, equipment and consent checklist |
| Guided foundations | Build shared vocabulary and skills | Baseline exercises |
| Project cycles | Prototype, test, debug and explain | Portfolio evidence and mentor feedback |
| Review | Demonstrate understanding | Rubric-based feedback and guardian summary where appropriate |
| Progression | Choose next challenge or pause | Personalized recommendation with no pressure |

## Learning loop

**Explain → Demonstrate → Practice → Debug → Reflect → Present.** Use this loop within lessons and across multi-week projects.

## Parent experience

Provide a clear program brief; supervision and communications policy; consent and privacy options; realistic milestones; attendance and feedback records; and an accessible escalation path.

## Friction to eliminate

Confusing prerequisites, expensive surprise hardware, unclear software installation, long passive lectures, absent feedback, difficult scheduling, and vague claims of progress.

## Design targets, not claims

A timely response to parent queries, repeatable setup for most learners, visible project milestones, and supportive mentor reviews. Establish measured service-level objectives during pilots.
