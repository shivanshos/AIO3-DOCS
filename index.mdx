# Welcome to AIO3

AIO3 is an India-first AI and STEM skill-development initiative exploring premium, mentor-led, project-based education for students at different readiness levels.

We focus on **understanding systems**, **learning through building**, and **responsible use of technology**. This documentation is an evolving project resource, not a finalized course catalogue.
