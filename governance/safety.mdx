# Trust and safeguarding

AIO3 may serve minors. Safeguarding, privacy and age-appropriate instruction are design requirements—not optional marketing features.

## Baseline policy proposals

1. Establish verified educator roles, training requirements and reporting channels.
2. Obtain necessary guardian permissions and explain the activity, data use and communication channels.
3. Collect only essential personal information and define retention/deletion policies.
4. Do not publish children's names, images, voices, work or contact details without appropriate permissions.
5. Keep private learner data out of public GitHub, unapproved AI platforms and model prompts.
6. Supervise electronics, power equipment, robotics, drones and network security activities.
7. Use approved devices, accounts and software with least-privilege access.
8. Require human oversight for safety-sensitive or personally consequential AI outputs.
9. Maintain incident-response, escalation and communication procedures.
10. Verify applicable Indian legal requirements with qualified counsel before program launch.

## Responsible AI learning

Students should learn the difference between plausible text and verified evidence, hallucination, personal-data leakage, prompt injection, tool permissions, bias, model licensing and human accountability.

## Physical and cyber labs

Use simulated robotics or constrained test rigs before real machines. Do not teach destructive network tests against systems without authorization. Back up data before systems exercises; ensure accessible emergency stops where physical movement is involved.

## Status

This is a **planning baseline**, not a completed legal compliance assessment or institution-approved child-safeguarding policy.
