# Operations model

AIO3 is exploring hybrid physical and online delivery with close mentorship. Standardized operational checks should protect the learning experience while allowing personalization.

## Before launch

- Define cohort purpose, prerequisites, instructor competence and maximum safe group size.
- Approve curriculum objectives, project rubrics and lesson plans.
- Check space, equipment, software licensing, accessibility and emergency readiness.
- Publish accurate scope, contact details, policies and fee/refund conditions.
- Set age-appropriate safeguarding, guardian consent and incident escalation procedures.

## Learner intake

Capture only necessary information: goals, readiness, accessibility needs volunteered for accommodation, guardian consent where required, and contact preferences. Restrict access according to role and retention policy.

## Session workflow

1. Mentor reviews the session plan and equipment.
2. Check attendance and any safety issues.
3. Introduce a clear objective and short demonstration.
4. Conduct guided/independent building.
5. Record learning observations without unnecessary personal detail.
6. Review project artifacts and assign bounded next steps.
7. Report meaningful updates to learners and guardians where appropriate.

## Capacity planning

A staffing model should account for instructional time, setup, parent communication, evaluation, maintenance, and support—not just live teaching hours.

## Quality assurance

Track environment uptime, lost instruction time, feedback turnaround, attendance, learner understanding and incident resolution. Review pilot results before scaling.
